excerpt: 'This research paper delves into the application of YOLO deep convolutional neural network (CNN) architectures for object detection in military surveillance. The performance of YOLOv3, YOLOv4, and YOLOv5m is compared on a publicly available military object detection dataset comprising of six classes (truck, tank, fighter jet, helicopter, car, and airplane). The experimental results showed that YOLOv5m achieved higher performance, with precision, recall, and mAP@0.5 values at 91.2%, 94.4%, and 95.9% respectively. Next, the previous dataset is expanded by introducing four additional classes (person, gun, knife, and drone) that frequently appear in military surveillance scenarios. YOLOv5m trained on this dataset achieved precision, recall, and mAP@0.5 values of 83.5%, 79.4%, and 82.3% respectively. Finally, Slicing Aided Hyper Inference (SAHI) technique is applied to improve the performance of YOLOv5m in detecting small object instances located far away within a scene.'
