excerpt: 'Planning a trip typically involves searching for tourist destinations, and travel blogs are a rich source of information for such destinations. However, these blogs typically contain an extensive amount of text and descriptions of multiple tourist attractions. Named Entity Recognition (NER) can be used to help users in extracting tourist destinations from lengthy travel narratives. In this work, a BERT-BiLSTM-CRF NER model is proposed that combines contextualized word embeddings from BERT, BiLSTM for capturing sequential information, and CRF for assigning labels to sequential data.'
